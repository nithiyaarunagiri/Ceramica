Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Made for Life

We believe that the heart of every home is the kitchen. Our ceramic cookware is designed to bring warmth, beauty, and functionality to your daily rituals.

Since 2018

Our Journey Began in a Small Kitchen

What started as a passion for beautifully crafted cookware has grown into a brand loved by home cooks around the world. Our founder, Sarah, spent years searching for ceramic pieces that combined timeless design with everyday durability — and when she couldn't find them, she decided to create them herself.

She began with a single kiln in a small London studio, hand-throwing Dutch ovens and baking dishes for friends and family. Word spread quickly — first through local markets, then through food bloggers and home cooks who fell in love with the warm, earthy finishes and the way our pieces cooked.

Today, Ceramica is a team of artisans, designers, and food lovers dedicated to bringing you cookware that performs beautifully and looks stunning on your table. Every piece is still touched by hand, still glazed in small batches, and still made with the same care as the very first pot Sarah pulled from that kiln.

— Sarah Mitchell, Founder

How It's Made

From raw clay to your kitchen table — every piece follows four careful steps.

01

Sourcing the Clay

We hand-select natural clay from trusted, sustainable quarries — free from lead, cadmium, and other harmful additives.

02

Shaping & Firing

Each piece is shaped by our master artisans, then fired at over 1,200°C to develop strength, density, and a durable finish.

03

Hand Glazing

Our signature earthy glazes are applied by hand in small batches — giving every piece a subtly unique, one-of-a-kind tone.

04

Quality & Packing

Every piece is inspected by hand and packed in recyclable, plastic-free materials — ready to bring warmth to your kitchen.

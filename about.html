Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Made for Life

We believe that the heart of every home is the kitchen. Our ceramic cookware is designed to bring warmth, beauty, and functionality to your daily rituals.

Since 2018

Our Journey Began in a Small Kitchen

What started as a passion for beautifully crafted cookware has grown into a brand loved by home cooks around the world. Our founder, Sarah, spent years searching for ceramic pieces that combined timeless design with everyday durability — and when she couldn't find them, she decided to create them herself.

Today, Ceramica is a team of artisans, designers, and food lovers dedicated to bringing you cookware that performs beautifully and looks stunning on your table.

— Sarah Mitchell, Founder

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that shape everything we do.

Our Mission

To create ceramic cookware that brings joy to everyday cooking — combining timeless design, sustainable materials, and exceptional performance for kitchens everywhere.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in ceramic cookware, inspiring a global community of home cooks to embrace the art of slow, intentional cooking with beautiful, enduring pieces.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped who we are today.

2018

The Beginning

Ceramica was founded in a small London kitchen with a single kiln and a dream to create the perfect ceramic Dutch oven.

2020

First Collection

Launched our first full collection of cookware, bakeware, and storage — instantly loved by home cooks and food bloggers.

2022

Global Expansion

Expanded to 35 countries with a dedicated team of artisans, designers, and customer care specialists.

2024

New Chapter

Launched our sustainable packaging initiative and introduced our first eco-friendly production line.

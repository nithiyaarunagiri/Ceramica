Material Deep Dive

Everything you need to know about ceramic, cast iron, and stainless steel.

Ceramic

Our Signature

Non-toxic, naturally non-stick, and beautifully crafted. Ceramic cookware is our specialty — offering even heat distribution and a timeless aesthetic.

  • 100% PFOA & lead-free
  • Naturally non-stick surface
  • Even heat distribution
  • Oven & dishwasher safe
  • Available in earthy tones
Shop Ceramic

Cast Iron

Classic Choice

Exceptional heat retention and durability. Cast iron is perfect for slow cooking, searing, and baking — a true kitchen workhorse.

  • Superior heat retention
  • Versatile for all cooking methods
  • Develops natural seasoning
  • Oven & stovetop safe
  • Heirloom quality
Shop Cast Iron

Stainless Steel

Modern Essential

Durable, sleek, and built to last. Stainless steel offers excellent heat conduction and is resistant to rust, stains, and corrosion.

  • Durable & long-lasting
  • Rust & stain resistant
  • Excellent heat conduction
  • Oven safe up to 500°F
  • Modern, sleek design

Material Care Tips

Keep your cookware in perfect condition with these simple care guidelines.

Ceramic

Care Guide
  • ✅ Hand wash with warm soapy water and a soft sponge
  • ✅ Avoid metal utensils to preserve the non-stick surface
  • ✅ Let cool before cleaning
  • ✅ Dishwasher safe, but hand washing is recommended

Cast Iron

Care Guide
  • ✅ Hand wash only with warm water and a stiff brush
  • ✅ Avoid soap — it strips the natural seasoning
  • ✅ Dry immediately after washing
  • ✅ Apply a thin layer of oil after each use

Stainless Steel

Care Guide
  • ✅ Dishwasher safe, but hand washing preserves shine
  • ✅ Use stainless steel cleaner for stubborn spots
  • ✅ Avoid abrasive scouring pads to prevent scratching
  • ✅ Dry with a soft cloth for streak-free finish
